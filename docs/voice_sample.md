# Voice profile (build.md 13.1, the voice sample placeholder)

Drawn from a writing sample Luke supplied on 2026-10-23 (a personal application letter). The letter itself is not in this repository because it holds personal details; this profile describes how Luke writes and holds no personal content (D-616).

## How Luke writes

- **First person, active voice.** He says what he did and why: "I have chosen", "I am eager to", "I completed". In technical writing this becomes "I built", "I measured", "I chose".
- **Direct statements of reasons.** He gives the reason plainly and moves on, often naming the alternatives he weighed and why he chose one.
- **Concrete facts with dates and numbers.** He backs claims with specifics (years, counts, named courses) rather than adjectives.
- **Medium-length sentences** (roughly 15 to 25 words), one idea each, with occasional short lists.
- **Earnest and positive, not boastful.** He says what interests him and what he wants to do next.
- **He describes his own approach as rational and straightforward**, so the writing should read as reasoned and plain.

## What to keep out (build.md 13.1 wins where the sample differs)

The sample uses some words the brief must not: "cutting-edge", "robust", "Furthermore", "Moreover", "groundbreaking", "prestigious". Keep Luke's directness and first person; replace these with plain words or start a new sentence. Keep his concreteness; drop praise adjectives.

## Applying it

- Brief, README and cover note: first person where natural ("I"), short paragraphs, every claim with a number from `results/`, the reason for each choice in one sentence.
- Limitations and FAQ: the same plain voice; say what failed and by how much, without softening.
